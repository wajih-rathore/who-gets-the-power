# Who Gets the Power?: 3-minute video script

Read aloud by the team over the six stages of the demo, about 230 spoken words (about 2 minutes, leaving room for the map animations). The narration is the team's own wording; keep it word for word. One data fix in stage 2: "everyone else is fine" became "the others mostly stay lit", because under today's rule Gulshan-e-Iqbal is also dark at 3. Numbers come from `web/data/results.json` (normal scenario). This story, its places and its numbers are made up for the demo. Open the page at stage 1: https://who-gets-the-power.vercel.app/#step=1

## ① The shortfall
[SCREEN: Stage 1. Power flows from the power station; the plugs per hour grey out.]

"It's a hot afternoon in Karachi, and everyone switches on their fans at once. The power station can't keep up: there's only enough electricity for 3 of our 4 places at 2 o'clock, and just 2 of them at 3. Someone has to wait in the dark."

## ② Today's rule
[SCREEN: Stage 2. Lyari's line cuts at 2, 3 and 4.]

"Today the rule is simple: switch off the same neighbourhood first, every time. So Lyari's lights go out at 2… and at 3… and at 4. Three hot hours in a row, while the others mostly stay lit. That doesn't feel fair."

## ③ Switches → qubits
[SCREEN: Stage 3. The 12-switch grid.]

"Let's think of it as switches. One switch for each place, for each hour: 4 places × 3 hours = 12 switches. Flip them every possible way and you get 4,096 different plans. A quantum computer gives each switch its own qubit."

## ④ Quantum search
[SCREEN: Stage 4. The "23× better than guessing" bar and the small curve.]

"Instead of checking plans one at a time, the quantum computer plays with all 12 switches together. Round after round, 42 rounds in all, it learns which plans are fair and makes them come up more often, like a game of hot and cold. By the end, it finds the best plan 23 times more often than guessing."

## ⑤ Double-check
[SCREEN: Stage 5. The check badge.]

"Then an ordinary computer checks the slow way, trying all 4,096 plans one by one. Same answer. ✓"

## ⑥ The fair plan
[SCREEN: Stage 6. The plan plays across the hours; then press "What if a power station breaks down?"]

"Now everyone takes a short, fair turn. No neighbourhood is dark for more than an hour, nobody goes dark twice in a row, the hospital always stays on, and the big AI computer centre takes a break at 4 to help out."
