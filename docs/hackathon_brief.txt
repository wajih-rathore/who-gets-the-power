The Quantum Optimization Challenge
How can quantum computing be used to address a meaningful real-world optimization
problem with competing objectives or limited resources?

Challenge brief
Real-world decisions involve choosing the best option while working with limited time, resources, or capacity. Teams must select
ONE of the six application domains below, identify their own meaningful optimization problem, and develop a quantum or hybrid
quantum-classical approach using IBM Qiskit. There is no pre-assigned problem: each team defines its own use case within the
chosen domain.


What each team must do
01 Identify — a concrete, relevant optimization problem and its intended users or context.
02 Formulate — the decision variables, objective function, constraints, and a small test instance.
03 Justify — the selected quantum algorithm and show how the problem is represented in it.
04 Implement — and run a meaningful quantum component in Qiskit can be a tool or a subpart.
05 Evaluate — the result using a suitable classical, exact, or analytically verifiable reference.
06 Reflect — on correctness, feasibility, practical limitations, and any trade-offs observed.

Choose one of six application domains

Select one domain and define your own optimization problem. The examples below are only to help you get started; they are not
fixed problem statements. Use a small, simulator-friendly instance. Physical hardware and demonstrated quantum speedup are not
required.

  ELIGIBLE DOMAIN                          ILLUSTRATIVE OPTIMIZATION TASKS

  1. UAVs & Robotics                       Mission task allocation, inspection scheduling, or robot charging schedules
  2. Disaster Response                     Allocation of rescue teams, emergency supplies, or shelter resources
  3. Healthcare                            Patient appointment scheduling or allocation of limited medical equipment
  4. Agriculture                           Irrigation scheduling or assignment of equipment across fields
  5. Logistics & Transportation            Delivery assignments, vehicle scheduling, or distribution planning
  6. Energy & Smart Infrastructure         Power-use scheduling or allocation of charging slots


Deliverables
 ● A concise problem statement with objective, constraints, and motivation.
 ● Working Qiskit code and a reproducible demonstration on at least one small instance provide Github Repositories link.
 ● A brief report containing Architecture diagram ,results, a classical/exact reference comparison, and an honest discussion of
   limitations.
 ● A short technical pitch explaining the approach 3-min demo for evaluation ( Google Drive or link).
