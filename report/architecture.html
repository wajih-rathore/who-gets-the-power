Who Gets the Power? System architecture

Fair load-shedding during a power shortfall, solved with Qiskit QAOA and checked classically.

1Inputs

  • 4 areas: 3 neighbourhoods + an AI data centre that can pause
  • 3 peak hours
  • Shortfall per hour: 1, 2, 1 areas off
  • Hospital always on
→

2Problem builder (QUBO)

12 binary variables x[area,hour] = 1 if off. Rules become penalties:

  • Exact shortfall per hour (big penalty)
  • Fairness: squared dark hours
  • No back-to-back cuts
  • AI-centre revenue
  • Economic damage
→
3 · Quantum loop (Qiskit)

QQAOA circuit

  • 12 qubits, 2 layers
  • Runs on StatevectorSampler
↑angles
↓energy

CClassical optimizer

  • COBYLA tunes the 4 angles
  • 42 evaluations, 3 restarts
→

4Measure & decode

  • 1,024 shots
  • Bitstrings → plans
  • Keep the best plan that follows the rules
→

5Classical check & baselines

  • Brute force over all 4,096 plans gives the same optimum
  • Baselines: today's rule (highest loss first), greedy, AI-centre-first
→

6Output

results.json → web app, two screens:

  • Today's plan: power towers + Gantt
  • How quantum chose it: Set up → Tune → Measure → Check
