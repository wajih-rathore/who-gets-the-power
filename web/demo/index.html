How did quantum choose this? →

12 qubits = 12 on/off switches.

Each switch answers one question: is this area off at this hour? The city's rules become penalties, and the best plan is the one with the lowest total.

Wrong number of areas offbig penalty
Same area dark twice in a rowpenalty
Unfair share of dark hourspenalty
Pausing the AI centresmall cost
QAOA is made for yes/no problems with penalties, so it fits.

Tune: the optimizer explores, then settles on a low penalty.

A normal computer turns the circuit's 4 dials over 42 tries. Early tries jump around; it ends at an average penalty of 52.9, down from 124.6 at the start. Best of 3 restarts kept.
